# Mama — AFRITOON Character Bible

Mama is the warm, perceptive mother whose presence can turn an ordinary scene into a punchline.

## Visual identity

- Older Nigerian woman with a strong maternal silhouette.
- Medium-dark brown skin.
- Distinctive headwrap/hair treatment.
- Blouse with wrapper/comfortable home clothing and simple house shoes.
- Fuller, grounded silhouette that contrasts clearly with Tunde and Seyi.
- Clean 2D human-looking design with bold readable outlines and simple shading.
- Face and hands are deliberately expressive for close-up comedy.

## Comedy identity

Mama's strongest weapon is anticipation: she often appears to know what happened before anybody explains it.

Signature reactions:

1. Silent stare.
2. One eyebrow raised.
3. Hands on hips.
4. Knowing look.
5. Instant interrogation face.
6. Warm smile that suddenly becomes serious.
7. Slow turn toward the person who is in trouble.

The character should feel funny without reducing her to a stereotype. Her warmth, intelligence and authority are as important as the joke.

## Production asset contract

Artwork should be separable into:

`back_hair`, `legs`, `shoes`, `torso`, `left_arm`, `right_arm`, `neck`, `head`, `ears`, `front_hair`, `left_eye`, `right_eye`, `left_brow`, `right_brow`, `nose`, `mouth`.

Required views: `front`, `three_quarter`, `side`.

Required expressions: `neutral`, `happy`, `curious`, `shocked`, `deadpan`, `angry`, `sad`, `laughing`, `surprised`.

Required action coverage: idle, walk, sit, stand, talk, point, look, turn, wave, laugh, cry, shock, angry, surprise, dance, vibe, freeze, check_pocket, shrug, look_at_camera.

The final artwork must be original and must not reproduce a real person's likeness or copyrighted character.
