# ITANRA Brand Direction

## Working brand

**ITANRA**

## Positioning

**Stories that feel like home.**

ITANRA is a storytelling and animation production engine focused on relatable African short-form stories.

The automation is the production advantage. The audience experiences the stories, characters and humor—not the machinery behind them.

## First universe

**Tunde, Seyi & Mama**

- Tunde — the confident mistake.
- Seyi — the deadpan human reaction button.
- Mama — the final boss of information.

## Naming architecture

- Brand/studio: ITANRA
- Technology: ITANRA Engine
- Character universe: Tunde, Seyi & Mama
- Episodes: independent titles such as `NEPA, Please!`
- Future: additional characters, shows and story worlds can exist under ITANRA.

## Visual direction

Human-looking stylized 2D characters. Avoid photorealism and avoid generic procedural figures. Characters should be expressive, recognizable, culturally grounded and easy to animate consistently.

## Content principle

REAL-LIFE EXPERIENCE → EMOTION → RELATABLE SCENE → TWIST/PUNCHLINE → PAYOFF

## Production principle

**Build → render → watch → improve → publish.**

## Brand safety

Do not imply legal trademark clearance from this working name. Before commercial registration, perform formal trademark, company, domain and social-handle clearance in the target markets.
